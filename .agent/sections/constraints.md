# Constraints

During a hackathon, time is your most valuable resource. Work within these constraints to ensure you ship.

### 1. Time-Bound
- **MVP First:** Don't spend more than 4 hours on a single feature. If it's not working, pivot or simplify.
- **Hard Deadlines:** Your code must be on GitHub and your program on Devnet at least 1 hour before the deadline.

### 2. Scope-Bound
- **One Clear Problem:** Solve one thing well rather than five things poorly.
- **Feature Freeze:** Stop adding new features 6 hours before the deadline. Spend the remaining time on UI/UX polish and the demo video.

### 3. Technical Constraints
- **Mainnet Costs:** Be mindful of on-chain data storage costs. Store only what's necessary (proofs, scores, identities).
- **Network Congestion:** Always implement retries and clear error messages for failed transactions.
